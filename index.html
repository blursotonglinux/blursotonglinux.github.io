"It is only with the heart that one can see rightly;
what is essential is invisible to the eye." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Linux is the water we all swim in. It runs most of the world's servers and everyday electronics: invisible because it's everywhere, essential because nothing moves without it.

BLUR is a free, volunteer-led weekly programme based in Singapore that introduces youths and adults from non-tech backgrounds to Linux and its open-source playground: to notice the water around them, recognise their ability to choose, and swim their own paths through the currents.
